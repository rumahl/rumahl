may not be used to identify a modified or redistributed version without

## What requires permission

- Using **rumahl** or the rumahl logomark as the name or branding of a modified

- Using the marks in a way that suggests sponsorship, affiliation, or
  endorsement by the rumahl project.
- Registering trademarks, domains, app-store names, or social handles that are
  confusingly similar to the marks.

## Forks and redistributions

A fork is welcome, but to distribute a modified build it must:

1. Rename the product and remove **rumahl** from its name and UI.
2. Replace the rumahl logomark and brand-specific artwork.
3. Keep the Apache License 2.0 and asset notices intact.
4. Not imply that it is the official rumahl OS.

Technical identifiers that are not branding — crate/package names such as
`rumahl-core`, protocol identifiers, and file paths — may remain if they are
needed for compatibility.

## Relationship to the asset license

The license declared for an asset in the multi-license
[`rumahl/assets`](https://github.com/rumahl/assets) repository governs
**copying and adapting** first-party artwork. This trademark policy governs the
**source-identifying** use of the marks. The two are complementary: you may not
use the logo to brand a fork, even where the asset license would otherwise
permit copying the artwork.
