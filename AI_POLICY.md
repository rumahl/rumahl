# Rumahl AI Policy

AI tools, such as large language models and AI-assisted development tools, are permissible for use in Rumahl projects.

AI is regarded as a development tool rather than a replacement for human accountability. Contributors are accountable for their submissions, while maintainers are responsible for the work they review, merge, and release.

All contributions must adhere to the same standards, irrespective of whether AI was involved in their creation.

## Human Responsibility

Any AI-assisted or AI-generated contribution must be reviewed and comprehended by the submitting individual.

Contributors should be able to articulate the purpose, functionality, and implications of their changes, as well as respond to inquiries without relying on AI systems for assistance.

Unreviewed or poorly understood AI-generated contributions may be denied.

## Autonomous Agents

AI agents and other automated systems must not submit contributions, open issues, engage in discussions, or respond to reviews independently unless explicitly permitted by a Rumahl project.

A human must retain responsibility for actions taken on their behalf and must review generated content prior to submission.

Automated activities that create unnecessary workload for maintainers, circumvent contribution processes, or yield unreviewed content may be removed or classified as spam.

## Code, Tests, and Documentation

AI tools may assist with code, tests, documentation, configuration, refactoring, debugging, and other development tasks.

Generated outputs must be verified for correctness, security, maintainability, licensing issues, and consistency with the project.

Utilizing AI does not diminish the level of review expected from contributors.

AI-assisted autocomplete and similar development tools are considered standard tools and do not require disclosure by default.

Projects may opt to disclose more significant use of generated code or other AI-produced content when such disclosure provides useful context.

## Issues, Pull Requests, and Reviews

AI tools may be employed to enhance grammar, structure, translation, or clarity in project communications.

However, the essence of an issue, pull request, review, or technical discussion should reflect the contributor's own understanding and intent.

Do not submit AI-generated responses that have not been personally reviewed.

When asked to explain or justify a contribution, contributors are expected to understand their work independently. AI may assist in refining the explanation, but it should not substitute the contributor's own reasoning.

## Disclosure

Routine use of tools such as AI-assisted autocomplete, grammar correction, or translation does not require disclosure.

When AI has significantly contributed to a submission or project, disclosure may be warranted. Individual Rumahl projects may establish additional disclosure requirements as needed.

Repositories may utilize labels, topics, documentation, or other metadata to indicate substantial AI involvement.

## AI Used by Rumahl

Rumahl projects may utilize AI-assisted tools for tasks such as code review, issue triage, testing, documentation, analysis, or project maintenance.

AI-generated suggestions are not definitive and may contain inaccuracies. Maintainers retain responsibility for decisions made based on these tools.

Human maintainers hold the final authority on project decisions.

## Enforcement

Contributions that do not comply with this policy may be rejected, closed, reverted, or removed.

Repeated misuse of AI tools, particularly through automated spam or knowingly submitting unreviewed generated content, may lead to restrictions on contributing to Rumahl projects.

Project-specific contribution guidelines may impose additional requirements beyond this policy.
